# Skidalyth menu  
<sub>Made by Chillzmodz</sub>

Skidalyth Menu is a **feature-packed** mod menu for Gorilla Tag, built by the community, for the community. Whether you just want mods, are a developer, or anything inbetween, this menu has you covered. Designed to be **as useful as possible**, it includes a variety of features and options that let you customize your modding experience to your heart’s content.  

<details>
  <summary><b>💡 Why not open-source?</b></summary>
	
Great question. We do not want People **skiding** Our Menu.  

<details>
  <summary><b>is this skidded?</b></summary>
No we gave credits.

Let's bring back the collaboration of modding. No paywalls, no secrets, no malware, just good mods.  


<details>
  <summary><b>💾 Installation</b></summary>
	
1. **Download** the latest release **[here](github.com/chillzmodz/skidalyth/releases/latest)**
2. **Drag & Drop** `Skidalyth.dll` into your plugins folder  
3. **Launch** Gorilla Tag and enjoy!
